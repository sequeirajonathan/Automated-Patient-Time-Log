{# The control centre. This replaced a dashboard that had grown seven panels nobody read: a scheduler for automation that was abandoned, an end-of-shift reconciliation against a handwritten log that is not kept, a "needs attention" list that was empty every day, and a mirror panel that reported what the controller thought it was doing rather than what the phone was showing. What is here instead is what somebody actually sits with this page open to do: · watch the phone, and watch what SHE sees of the phone, side by side, · read the screen with nothing folded away, to teach her the front end, · know who else has the portal open, · change how big the phone's text is, safely, · see whether the Pi and the phone are about to run out of something. Two rules it is built to: NO REDACTION. /app decides what is worth showing somebody standing in a patient's kitchen. This page decides nothing — it prints the tree. You cannot teach an interface through an interface that is quietly editing itself. MOBILE FIRST. It is opened from a bookmark on an iPhone at least as often as from a desk. One column, big targets, safe-area insets, and nothing that needs a pointer. Every visible string goes through t().
